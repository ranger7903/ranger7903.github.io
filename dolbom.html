Parent Care Log

Hospital trips, visits, medicine pick-ups, money spent — brothers and sisters write it down together, and the costs are shared fairly. For Android phones.

Not one line of anybody else’s advertising

  • Nothing covers the screen. At the very bottom there is one line: "Made by Valuestools".
  • It does not even ask for internet access. What you write stays on the phone.
  • No account, no server, no monthly fee.
Getting ready…

Open this page on the phone and press the button.

Once you have it

  1. Tap the downloaded file (Valuestools Parent Care Log.apk).
  2. If it asks about "apps from unknown sources", choose allow. — that is because it is not on the Play Store yet.
  3. Add the names of the family members who help, then press Start.

What it is for

  • "Who took Mum to the clinic last time?" — one line: date, who, what.
  • Write future dates too (next check-up, next prescription) and they show under Coming up.
  • See who paid how much this month, and who should send how much to whom to make it even.
  • Copy that summary straight into the family chat.

Keeping phones in step — without a server

Phones do not sync by themselves. Instead the app makes one line of share code: send it in a message, the other person pastes it in, and both phones match. If two people changed the same entry, the later change wins.

What it does not do — written down plainly

It does not sync on its own, because there is no server.

A notification that pops up on its own is not in yet. Future dates are shown under Coming up when you open the app.

It says nothing about medicines or illnesses. It is a notebook, nothing more.
