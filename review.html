Tell us how it went

Whatever you find awkward here gets fixed in the next version. What we changed is written down on the Updates page.

We ask you for nothing

  • There is no box for your name, your phone, or your email. No account either.
  • We don't know who wrote this. Your IP address is not stored.
  • The owner reads every line himself. Nothing goes to an ad company.
  • We decide whether a review is shown on the site, but we never edit the words. A harsh one goes up as written, or not at all. Never rewritten.

What we do with your review

  1. When the same thing comes up more than once, that is the real problem. It goes first.
  2. We gather the fixes onto one page.
  3. Nothing is built until the owner presses "go ahead". It never happens on its own.
  4. When the new version is out we write it on the Updates page. The upgrade is free.

There are no ads in our things

Even in the apps we give away, there is not one line of anybody else's advertising. Nothing covers the screen. Nothing makes you wait five seconds. At the very bottom there is one line, "Made by Valuestools" — that is all of our advertising. Which is why we have no reason to track what you tapped, either.
