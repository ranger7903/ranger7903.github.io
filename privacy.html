1. 기본 원칙

밸류스의 모든 프로그램은 “여러분의 파일은 여러분의 컴퓨터를 떠나지 않는다”는 원칙으로 만들어졌습니다. 문서 정리, PDF 편집, 스크린샷 편집 같은 실제 작업은 전부 사용자의 컴퓨터 안에서만 일어나며, 그 내용을 밸류스의 서버로 전송하지 않습니다.

이 원칙은 휴대폰에서 쓰는 작은 웹 도구(암기 수첩, 오늘의 속담, 마음 서랍, 화장대 수첩)에도 그대로 적용됩니다. 적어 두신 내용은 그 기기 안에만 남고, 밸류스로 오지 않습니다.

2. 수집하는 정보

밸류스가 직접 수집하는 개인정보는 다음과 같이 최소한입니다.

  • 프로그램·구독 구매 시: 전자우편 주소, 결제 처리에 필요한 정보. 카드 번호 자체는 Paddle이 처리하며 밸류스는 보지도, 보관하지도 않습니다. 밸류스는 Paddle로부터 주문 내역과 전자우편 주소, 청구서에 필요한 국가 정보를 전달받습니다.
  • 책을 편지로 주문하실 때: 주문 편지를 보내 주시면 그 편지에 담긴 것이 그대로 수집됩니다. 곧 보내시는 분의 전자우편 주소와, 편지에 직접 적어 주신 이름이나 호칭, 그리고 주문하신 책 이름입니다. 책을 보내 드리려면 받을 주소(전자우편)가 필요하기 때문입니다. 값을 치르는 방법을 주고받는 과정에서 알려 주시는 정보도 여기에 포함됩니다.
  • 문의 시: 보내주신 전자우편 주소와 문의 내용.

맛보기 PDF를 내려받거나 무료 프로그램·무료 웹 도구를 쓰실 때는 가입도, 이름도, 전자우편 주소도 받지 않습니다.

3. 결제와 국외 이전

카드 결제는 국내·해외를 가리지 않고 모두 Paddle(Paddle.com Market Limited, 영국)이 처리합니다. 밸류스가 따로 쓰는 국내 결제대행사는 없습니다. Paddle은 기록상의 판매자(Merchant of Record)로서 자체 개인정보처리방침에 따라 결제 정보를 처리합니다.

Paddle이 영국 회사이므로, 카드로 결제하시면 개인정보가 국외로 넘어갑니다. 아래와 같이 알려 드립니다.

  • 이전받는 곳: Paddle.com Market Limited (영국)
  • 이전되는 항목: 전자우편 주소, 결제 카드 정보, 청구지 국가, 주문 내역
  • 이전되는 때와 방법: 결제 창에서 결제하시는 그 순간, 암호화된 통신으로
  • 이전하는 이유: 결제를 처리하고, 영수증을 보내고, 세금을 신고하고, 환불을 처리하기 위해서입니다.
  • 보관하는 기간: Paddle이 자사 방침과 영국·유럽의 법에 따라 정한 기간

국외 이전을 원치 않으시면 카드 결제를 하지 않으시면 됩니다. 다만 그 경우 카드로 사는 상품은 구매하실 수 없습니다. 책은 편지로 주문하시므로 Paddle을 거치지 않습니다.

Privacy Policy

Effective: September 17, 2026

1. Our principle

Every VALUES program is built on one rule: your files never leave your computer. Actual work — organizing documents, editing PDFs, editing screenshots — happens entirely on your own device and is never uploaded to a VALUES server.

The same holds for the small web tools for your phone (암기 수첩, 오늘의 속담, 마음 서랍, 화장대 수첩). What you write in them stays on that device and never reaches us.

2. Information we collect

The personal information VALUES collects directly is minimal:

  • When you buy a program or the subscription: your email address and what payment processing requires. Card numbers themselves are handled by Paddle; VALUES never sees or stores them. Paddle passes us the order details, your email address and the country needed for invoicing.
  • When you order a book by email: whatever is in the email you send — that is, your email address, any name you write in it, and the book you are ordering. We need a delivery address (your email) to send the files. Anything you tell us while arranging payment is included too.
  • When you contact us: the email address and message you send.

Downloading a sample PDF, or using the free programs and free web tools, requires no account, no name and no email address.

3. Payment and transfer abroad

All card payments, Korean and international alike, are processed by Paddle (Paddle.com Market Limited, United Kingdom). There is no separate Korean payment provider. Paddle acts as Merchant of Record and handles payment data under its own privacy policy.

Because Paddle is a UK company, paying by card transfers your personal information abroad. The details:

  • Recipient: Paddle.com Market Limited (United Kingdom)
  • What is sent: email address, card payment details, billing country, order details
  • When and how: at the moment you pay at the checkout, over an encrypted connection
  • Why: to process the payment, send a receipt, report tax, and handle refunds
  • How long Paddle keeps it: the period set by Paddle's own policy and UK/EU law

If you'd rather not have data transferred abroad, don't pay by card — though that means you can't buy the card-checkout products. Books are ordered by email and never go through Paddle.

4. How we use it

Your email address is used only to issue your licence key, deliver book files, confirm your purchase, manage your subscription, answer questions, and send occasional update notices. It is never used for marketing without your separate consent.

5. Retention

"As long as the law requires" tells you nothing, so here are the actual periods.

  • Contract and withdrawal records: 5 years (E-Commerce Act)
  • Payment records: 5 years (E-Commerce Act)
  • Complaint and dispute records: 3 years (E-Commerce Act)
  • Tax records and books: 5 years (Framework Act on National Taxes)
  • Ordinary enquiry emails: deleted within 1 year of our reply
  • Records remaining after a subscription is cancelled: deleted once the periods above end

Once a period ends, the information is destroyed without delay.

6. How we destroy it

Information past its retention period, or no longer needed, is checked by the owner and destroyed.

  • Procedure: the owner reviews what is held at least once a year and destroys whatever has expired.
  • Electronic files: deleted by a method that cannot be reversed, including the emails held in the mailbox.
  • Anything printed on paper: shredded.

7. Your rights and how to use them

You may, at any time:

  • ask to see what we hold about you,
  • ask us to correct anything wrong,
  • ask us to delete it,
  • ask us to stop processing it.

There's one way to do it: email ranger7903@nate.com. You may act through a representative. We handle the request and reply within 10 days. Where a record must be kept by law we cannot delete it, and we will tell you in the reply which law requires it.

If you are unhappy with the outcome, you can contact Korea's Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972), the Privacy Infringement Report Centre (118), the Supreme Prosecutors' Office (1301) or the National Police Agency (182).

8. Sharing

We do not sell or share your personal information with third parties, except where required by law. The only data that goes to Paddle is what is listed in section 3.

9. How we keep it safe

As much as a one-person shop can:

  • only one person — the owner — handles personal information; it is not delegated to anyone,
  • the mailbox and accounts holding purchase records are kept behind two-factor authentication,
  • the site and the download links are served only over an encrypted connection (HTTPS),
  • information we don't need is never collected in the first place, which we think is the surest protection of all,
  • we never ask for a Korean resident registration number, under any circumstances.

10. The chat helper on this site

A few pages have a small chat helper you can talk to. It runs entirely inside your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to VALUES or anywhere else. The only network request it ever makes is to read this site's own price files. Close the window and the conversation is gone.
