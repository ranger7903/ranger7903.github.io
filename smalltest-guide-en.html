🧪 Before you spend the money

By the day you first show months of solo preparation to anyone, you have already spent the money and energy you would need to change it. Here is how to try it once, first, at a size where failing costs nothing.

What the real problem is

We usually start something new like this: prepare alone for months, put up the sign, spend the money — and only then show it to anyone. But that first showing is already a day you can’t take back. If people shrug, the money and strength to fix it are gone.

What comedians do — the small stage

Before a big tour, the comedian Chris Rock is known to turn up unannounced at small clubs to try new material. He goes on with a notebook, crosses out what doesn’t get a laugh and keeps what does — for months, before the big stage.

What matters: on that small stage, failing changes nothing. Thirty people, nobody filming, come back next week.

The one line

Don’t weigh “will it work?” in your head. Actually do it once, at a size where failing is fine.

How small is “small enough to fail”

WhatSmall means
MoneySo little you won’t mind if it never comes back. Usually under 5% of the big plan
TimeYou see a result within a week. Two months is already the main stage
PrideSomewhere people you know won’t see. No one to tell the story of the flop

Five examples

Opening a shop? Main stage: lease a space, prepare three months, open Small stage: one day at a weekend market (a small pitch fee) → How many stop? What do they pay? What do they ask? Making something to sell? Main stage: make a hundred and sell them Small stage: make three and show them to people who don't know you → Asking the price is a good sign. “That's nice” is not. Starting a course or qualification? Main stage: sign up for six months Small stage: three hours of free sample lessons → If you can't finish three hours, you won't finish six months. Thinking about a new line of work? Main stage: quit and switch Small stage: ask someone in that job for a coffee → After three of these, “not what I thought” always comes up. Starting writing or video? Main stage: launch a named channel and promote it Small stage: post five pieces without your name → Was it fun to make? After five, do you want to make more?

The surprise of one market day: customers ask about different things from the ones you prepared for. Learning that before you open is what this is for.

What to watch on the small stage — not praise

  • Did they pay? Even a little money is worlds apart from “nice.”
  • Did they ask on their own? What they asked before you explained is real interest.
  • Did they come back? Once is politeness. The second time is the answer.

Three common mistakes

  • Dressing up the small stage — it should be rough. Polish costs money and time, and then you can’t let it go.
  • Showing only friends and family — they like you, so they like it. Five strangers beat fifty friends.
  • Deciding after one try — once might be the weather. Try three times. Three shrugs is an answer.

Failing on the small stage means the method worked. Count it this way: “how many times did I go on a small stage this year?” — not how many went well.
