Why isn't the bank account printed here?

A bank number sitting on a public page lets somebody impersonate us and collect the money. So we send it by reply only. Our mail always comes from ranger7903@nate.com — anything else is not us.

4 · Tax documents — please read first

We cannot issue a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서).

VALUES is registered as a simplified taxpayer (간이과세자), and Korean law does not allow a simplified taxpayer to issue one. We say so here, before you file anything, rather than after your purchase request has been approved.

What we can give you instead:

  • Statement of account (거래명세서) — stamped, sent straight away.
  • Cash receipt for expense proof (지출증빙용 현금영수증) — issued for the amount transferred.

Tell us in your email which you need. If your accounting office requires a tax invoice specifically, please check with them first whether an expense-proof cash receipt is accepted.
