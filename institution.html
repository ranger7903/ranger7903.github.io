Why this page exists

Administrative offices cannot just buy. They go in one order: get a quote → file a purchase request → get it approved → pay with the company card. If getting the quote costs a day of waiting, the request cannot be filed that day. So we let you print the quote yourself — at night, on a weekend, whenever.

3 · Four steps

One. Print the quote above and attach it to your purchase request.

Two. Get it approved inside your organization. Nothing needed from us.

Three. Once approved, press «Pay for these by card» at the bottom of this page. Company cards work. The items and quantities you picked go straight into the checkout.

Four. When payment is done, the licence key and the card receipt arrive by email straight away. Use the key on the download page.

Why your card statement says «Paddle»

Card payments are handled by Paddle, a payment company in the United Kingdom. So the receipt and the card statement show Paddle, not VALUES. The checkout shows the price in your own currency. If your company card blocks overseas payments, ask your card company to allow it first.

4 · Tax documents — please read first

We cannot issue a Korean tax invoice (세금계산서).

VALUES is registered as a simplified taxpayer (간이과세자), and Korean law does not allow a simplified taxpayer to issue one. Paddle, which takes the card payment, does not issue one either. We say so here, before you file anything, rather than after your purchase request has been approved.

What we can give you instead:

  • Card receipt — sent to your email as soon as payment is done.
  • Statement of account (거래명세서) — email us your order number after paying and we send it stamped.

If your accounting office requires a tax invoice specifically, please check with them first whether a company card receipt is accepted.

5 · Questions we get

More than twenty computers?

Then we stop counting machines and price the organization as a whole. Email us roughly how many, and we set it up so you can pay that price by card.

Our forms are unusual — will it work?

Send one file by email. We run it for real and tell you. If it fails we fix it. Check before you buy.

What if it doesn't fit after all?

Full refund within 7 days if the licence key has not been activated; and any time at all if it will not install or does not match the description. See the refund policy.

Our network is closed — no internet.

That is fine. VALUES tools run entirely on the machine and send nothing out.
