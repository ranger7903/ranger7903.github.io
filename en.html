🔵 If a blue window appears during install — nothing is broken

When you double-click the file you downloaded, Windows may stop you once with a blue window saying “Windows protected your PC” (some versions say “Windows has protected your PC”). It does not mean there is anything wrong with the program.

1 Click “More info” in that blue window.
The text is small — it sits in the middle of the window.
2 A “Run anyway” button then appears below. Click it and the install starts.

Why does it happen?
Think of a shop that just opened on your street. Not a bad shop — simply one not many people have walked into yet, so Windows says “I don't know this place yet”. Big companies pay every year for a signing certificate that tells Windows “this shop is fine”. We put that money into making the tools instead. As more people download, the warning fades away on its own.
