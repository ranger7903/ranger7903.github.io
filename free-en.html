🔵 If a blue window appears when you install — nothing is broken

When you double-click the file, a blue window may appear once. Windows says “Windows protected your PC” and stops it for a moment. It is not because something is wrong with the program.

1 Click “More info” inside the blue window.
The text is small — it is about halfway down the window.
2 A “Run anyway” button appears at the bottom. Click it and the installer starts.

Why does it appear?
Like a new shop on the street: not a bad shop, just one not many people have visited yet, so Windows marks it as “I don’t know this one.” Big companies pay every year for a code-signing certificate; we spend that money on making the programs. As more people download, the warning goes away by itself.
